## When and why AI

Our philosophy is that AI should do the work that you can't or don't want to do. Transcribing audio is a great example: it's a slow, laborious process that's easy to get wrong. We give you the option to offload that work to the machines.

We also believe AI is a powerful accessibility feature. For instance, blind podcasters can have a difficult time preparing cover art for their shows. Having the option to generate simple, tasteful artwork can help these folks overcome an annoying but necessary obstacle to getting their show set up.

## Responsible choices

We prefer to use only what we need and nothing more. Most tasks don't require massive frontier models from the top AI labs: using open models allows us to choose vendors that prioritize privacy and energy use over sheer performance.

### No training, full stop.

We don't train AI models on your content. Never have, never will. Your content isn't for sale, and we're not trying to make more of it without you.

We stand by this: we do our diligence to ensure that all of the vendors we use to host your show comply with this policy as well: no training, full stop.

### You have the choice

Some features on Pinecast use AI, like transcriptions. If you don't choose to run them, they don't get used. You're always presented with a choice.

Note: We use AI features behind the scenes to detect spam and protect our services from abuse. We choose vendors that handle your data responsibly, and it's not provided with any information that isn't otherwise made public.

### AI behind the scenes

Pinecast uses AI to check new episodes for spam and AI-generated audio. On a free account, a check can suspend the account automatically. Paid accounts are never suspended automatically. If your account was suspended and you think it's a mistake, email support@pinecast.com and a person will review it.

These checks are the only places where AI acts on its own. We see each automatic suspension as it happens, and we look into every mistake.

- The spam check sends the public text of a new podcast and its episodes, the domain of the owner's email address (never the address itself), the plan, and the sign-up date to gpt-oss-120b, an open model that Cloudflare runs on Workers AI.
- The AI-generated audio detector is an open-source model that runs on our own servers. Your audio isn't sent anywhere for it.

### What our vendors keep

AssemblyAI deletes the audio and the transcript about a day after it finishes. Cloudflare doesn't keep what we send to Workers AI once it has answered. Neither one trains AI models on it.

We keep your transcripts as part of your podcast, and feedback insights for 48 hours.

## How we manage the risks

We hold our use of AI to three rules:

- No critical or paid part of Pinecast relies on a decision made by AI by default.
- If that ever changes, we'll tell the people who use the feature about its risks, and remind them over time.
- AI never takes a destructive action, like deleting something for good or moving money.

These are the main risks we watch for, and what we do about each one.

<Table label="AI risks and what we do about them">

| Risk | What we do |
| --- | --- |
| A transcript gets words wrong | You can edit any transcript in your dashboard, or remove it. A podcast setting hides transcripts from listeners |
| Feedback insights misread your listeners, or suggest a change you shouldn't make | They run only when you ask, they're labeled as AI, each point is tied to something a listener said, and they're only advice: nothing acts on them |
| A free account is suspended by mistake | Only free accounts, and only when the check is confident. We see every suspension, and a person reviews any that you ask about |
| Someone hides instructions for the AI in podcast text | Our models can't use tools or reach any data. We read their answers as text, and never run them |
| Personal information reaches a model | Each feature sends only the fields it needs. Email addresses are removed, or cut down to the domain |
| A model changes without warning | We pin each model to an exact version, and change it the way we change any of our code |

</Table>

We can turn off any AI feature within minutes. We review this page, and the practice behind it, every year.
