# Your privacy is important

Starting a podcast means putting a live email address out into the world. Pinecast allows you to do that without revealing your personal email to listeners, and without making you manage a separate inbox.

Setting up email privacy is easy: opt-in by flipping a switch when setting up your show, or in your podcast settings. Enabling email privacy replaces the email address published in your feed with a forwarding address managed by Pinecast.

<DefinitionList>
    <dt>Without email privacy</dt>
    <dd>your-email@gmail.com</dd>
    <dt>With email privacy</dt>
    <dd>your-show@listener.email</dd>
</DefinitionList>

## How does it work?

Once you opt-in, Pinecast sets up an email address on <code>listener.email</code> just for your show. There's no second step. We'll use that forwarding address anywhere an email is published.

When someone emails the address, we'll automatically forward it to the email we have on file for your account. Replying to messages replies from your personal email address.
