# Flexible billing options

Need flexibility with how you plan to grant access and use our service? No problem.

We offer two options:

1. **One admin account** with access granted on a per-podcast basis. One account owns your content and other accounts can be granted access at no cost.
2. **Seat-based pricing** that applies your subscription across multiple accounts. Give each student or faculty member their own paid account to own their own content.

Add-on packages, like our Growth, Hi-Fi, and Pro Analytics packages, are available for an additional fee. These grant additional features.

Volume-based discounts are available for seat-based pricing. Discounts are available for all plans with multi-year commitments.

# AI you can actually control

Your students and faculty are concerned about AI. We are too.

Pinecast never trains AI models on your content. All of our AI features are clearly labeled and can always be turned off. We document all of our AI features on our [AI use page](/ai-use).

[Reach out](mailto:support@pinecast.com) if you have questions about AI at Pinecast.

# Accessible to creators and listeners

We treat [accessibility](/features/accessibility) extremely seriously. Podcasting is the most accessible form of media, and we work hard to make sure that it stays that way.

Here are some of the ways that we make podcasting more accessible:

- Everything you interact with on Pinecast is tested for accessibility: dashboards, players, podcast websites, short links, and more. Accessibility is built in, not bolted on.
- Every change goes through an accessibility review, and our code has hundreds of accessibility tests.
- [Transcripts](/features/transcripts) make it easier for folks that are deaf and hard of hearing to consume your media.
- Features like generative AI cover art make it possible for blind podcasters to satisfy the minimum requirements for a valid podcast feed.

# Only as much data as needed

Privacy is important to us. It's our policy never to collect more data than we need to. There's no spying, we don't sell your data or your listeners' data, and we retain usage data for only as long as it's needed.

Our [podcast analytics](/features/analytics) use privacy-preserving techniques to provide robust, accurate analytics data while also protecting the privacy of your listeners.

See our list of [subprocessors](/subprocessors) to understand which vendors handle Pinecast user data.

# FAQs

## Can we pay by PO and invoice?

Yes. We send a quote, you send a PO, and we invoice against it.

## Can the service start on a set date?

Yes. Your year of service starts on the date you choose.

## What happens when a student graduates?

If you're using our seat-based billing, you can remove the student from your plan (freeing up the seat). They continue to own any content in their account, and can upgrade to one of our paid plans to continue distributing their show.

If you're not using seat-based billing, you can remove the student's access from your account. Your university account will continue to own the content.

## What if a payment runs late?

We understand that things don't always move as fast as we'd like. We offer a grace period of 60 days for payment. If the subscription fully lapses, no information is deleted: you can re-subscribe at any time.

## Can we move our shows from another host?

Yes. Pinecast [imports a show](/learn/import-a-podcast) from its feed.
