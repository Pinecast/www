# Billing that works with procurement

Pay the way your school pays its other vendors: from a quote, against a purchase order, by invoice.

- Pay by bank transfer, ACH debit, check or card
- A grace period when a payment runs late, with your shows online all along

# You decide who owns the shows

{/*
  Feature 3: flexible configuration for podcast ownership and administration.

## The school owns the shows

- One school account holds the shows of a department or a station
- Students and staff work on them as collaborators, with the Crew add-on
- Collaborators publish and edit, but can't delete or redirect a show
- Networks group a department's shows, also with Crew
- The Hi-Fi add-on, for bigger audio files

## Each student owns their show

- Each seat holder runs their own shows, in their own account
- A student keeps their show after they graduate, by moving it to a plan of their own

{/* Confirm what a student's account becomes when their seat ends. */}

## Either way

- One account manages the seats and sees the invoices
- Private episodes, for work that stays within a class

# AI that you can trust, and turn off

{/*
  Feature 5: responsible AI use and controls. Sources: the AI use page

  "Turn off": today, staff remove a feature from a school's seats one seat at
  a time (cover art generation, for example). A school has no switch for it in
  the dashboard. Feedback insights are a Growth feature, which a school plan
  does not have.

  The spam check and the AI-generated audio detector can suspend only free
  accounts. Confirm that both treat a seat as paid before saying the third
  point.

- Our AI vendors never train on your content
- AI runs only when someone asks for it, such as for a transcript or cover art
- No automated check suspends a school's account, and AI never deletes anything
- Ask us to turn AI features off for your school
- See every AI feature, its vendor and what it sends on our [AI use page](/ai-use)
